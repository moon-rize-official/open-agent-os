# EU / Germany Baseline

**Status:** INITIAL RESEARCH BASELINE  
**Date:** 2026-09-22

This is a research index, not legal advice.

## AI Act

Primary Commission source:
https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai

Current Commission materials indicate staged application, with enforcement/transparency provisions applying in 2026 and adjusted dates for high-risk categories following the 2026 simplification process.

Always re-check current Commission and EUR-Lex material before using dates in compliance logic.

## Germany AI Act implementation

Germany's KI-MIG:
https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ki-mig/

The law entered into force in July 2026 and sets national authority/enforcement arrangements for the EU AI Act.

https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2026/20260729_KI_VO.html
