# Legal / Compliance Scope for Agent Governance

**Domain:** 28 Agent Governance  
**Status:** RESEARCH IN PROGRESS

Governance must distinguish:

```text
What the agent CAN do
What policy ALLOWS it to do
What the organization WANTS it to do
What law/regulation REQUIRES or PROHIBITS
```

These are not interchangeable.

## Compliance context for agents

An agent task may receive a compliance pack selected by:

- jurisdiction;
- industry;
- actor role;
- organization profile;
- data classification;
- affected persons;
- task/action type;
- risk class.

## Example

A general coding task may require:

- license rules;
- secure-development policy;
- repository policy.

A recruiting workflow may additionally require:

- employment rules;
- privacy;
- AI high-risk classification analysis;
- human oversight;
- bias/fundamental-rights review.

The recruiting rules should not consume tokens in unrelated coding tasks.

The policy engine may enforce codified compliance controls, but legal applicability and interpretation must preserve uncertainty and escalation paths.
