# Code of Conduct

Open Agent OS is an evidence-focused technical community.

Contributors are expected to:

- engage with technical claims rather than attacking people;
- distinguish observations from proposals and opinions;
- cite sources when making factual or comparative claims;
- disclose conflicts of interest where relevant;
- respect privacy, licensing and responsible-disclosure boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing or deliberately disruptive behavior.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its contributors.

For sensitive conduct or security matters, use a private contact channel rather than a public issue.
